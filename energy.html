How this works

Value per energy = (Hard Cores gained × price per HC) ÷ energy spent. Price per HC defaults to an estimate of the final payout rate; fill in a prize pot and total HC below it to compute price per HC as pot ÷ total HC instead — handy for testing different end-of-event scenarios.
