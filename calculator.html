How this works. For your box cost, each player's spendable HC is floor(HC ÷ box cost) × box cost; everything below a full box — plus anyone under one box — is wasted. Summing every player's boxes gives the real total, so value per box = pot ÷ total boxes and your payout is your boxes × that value. Raising the box cost wastes more HC and shrinks the pool; "projected total HC" scales the whole leaderboard to model a bigger event.
